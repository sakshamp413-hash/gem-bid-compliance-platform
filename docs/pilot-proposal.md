# PRAMAAN Departmental Pilot Proposal
## AI-Powered Bid Compliance & Verification Infrastructure for Public Sector Procurement

**Target Partner:** Central Ministries, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), State Procurement Directorates  
**Engagement Type:** 90-Day Sandboxed Technical Pilot  
**Document Reference:** PRAMAAN-PILOT-2026-V1  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context
Public procurement evaluating teams face an overwhelming volume of complex multi-page statutory documents per tender. Manual cross-verification against statutory registries (GSTN, PAN, Udyam, MCA21) is labor-intensive, error-prone, and leaves evaluating officers legally exposed to audit disputes when fraudulent or collusive bids evade detection. At the same time, Indian MSMEs frequently suffer technical rejection due to clerical discrepancies.

**PRAMAAN** provides an automated, evidence-backed compliance verification and integrity layer that cuts technical evaluation time by over 80% while creating a mathematically verifiable, tamper-evident audit trail for every procurement decision.

---

## 2. Proposed Pilot Scope
The pilot will be conducted over a 90-day period covering a representative sample of 50 to 100 closed historical tenders and parallel evaluation of live tenders across two procurement categories.

### Key Deliverables of the Pilot
1. **Automated Tender Requirement Mapping:** Automatic extraction and structuring of eligibility criteria from tender notices and Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC).
2. **Statutory Document Verification:** Automated extraction and validation of GST, PAN, Udyam, MCA, and CA Turnover certificates.
3. **Forensic Document Integrity:** Byte-level inspection for post-signing PDF modifications, invalid digital signatures, and metadata tampering.
4. **Cross-Bidder Collusion Intelligence:** Automated detection of shared banking details, IFSC codes, and authorized signatories across competing bidders.
5. **Tamper-Evident Audit Dossier:** Single-click export of cryptographically signed compliance reports for vigilance and CAG audit defense.

## 4. Resource & Infrastructure Commitments

### Department Responsibilities
* Provide 50 anonymized historical tender documents and bid submissions for benchmarking.
* Nominate 3 to 5 evaluation officers for weekly feedback and user acceptance testing.
* Designate a technical point-of-contact for network and infrastructure coordination.

### PRAMAAN Team Responsibilities
* Deploy and maintain the sandboxed environment with 99.5% operational uptime.
* Conduct two training workshops for evaluating officers and vigilance personnel.
* Provide dedicated technical support with a 4-hour response SLA during business hours.
* Ensure all sensitive bidder data is encrypted at rest and in transit, with zero external cloud egress.

---

## 5. Success Metrics & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Turnaround Acceleration:** Minimum 75% reduction in time required to complete initial technical compliance reviews.
* **Accuracy & Consistency:** 100% agreement on mandatory statutory disqualifications compared to human committee consensus.
* **Integrity Detection:** Early identification of any forged documents, tampered PDFs, or shared banking credentials in the sample set.
* **Officer Usability:** >85% satisfaction rating on usability and clarity of the split-screen evidence viewer.
