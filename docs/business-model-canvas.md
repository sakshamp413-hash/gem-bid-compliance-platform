## 2. Revenue Models & Pricing Strategy

### 2.1 Government & Public Sector Licensing (B2G)
* **Tiered Departmental Subscription:** Fixed annual license covering an unlimited number of evaluating officers within a procurement directorate, scaled by annual procurement value.
* **Turnkey Deployment Support:** Dedicated implementation services for integrating with internal departmental ERP systems (SAP, Oracle) or private government cloud networks (NIC Cloud).

### 2.2 MSME Empowerment (Free Public Utility Model)
* **Free Public Readiness Portal:** Basic pre-submission verification (statutory checks, document validity checks, entity name matching) is provided free of charge to registered MSMEs to lower procurement barriers and foster inclusive participation.
* **Premium Self-Service Tiers (Post-Pilot):** Advanced pre-submission simulation (tender clause deep parsing and mock committee scoring) offered at nominal micro-subscription rates.
